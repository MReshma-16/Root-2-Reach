Root 2 Reach is a Tamil Nadu-focused e-commerce platform that connects **local artisans and sellers directly with customers**. The platform helps traditional products, handicrafts, handlooms, food products, and agricultural treasures reach a wider audience while preserving Tamil Nadu's cultural heritage.

---

## 🌟 About the Project

Tamil Nadu is rich in traditional crafts, agricultural products, handlooms, food products, and handmade items. However, many local artisans and small-scale sellers struggle to reach customers beyond their local markets.

**Root 2 Reach** provides a digital platform where:

👨‍🌾 **Sellers / Artisans** can showcase and sell their products.

🛍️ **Customers / Buyers** can discover, search, and purchase traditional products.

🌍 **Local products** can reach customers beyond their district.

🏺 **Traditional culture** can be promoted through digital commerce.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Many traditional artisans and small-scale producers in Tamil Nadu depend on local markets and middlemen to sell their products.

This creates several challenges:

- Limited customer reach
- Lack of digital presence
- Dependence on intermediaries
- Difficulty promoting traditional products
- Limited access to online markets
- Traditional products receiving less visibility

### 💡 Our Solution

Root 2 Reach creates a direct digital connection between **seller and customer**, allowing sellers to showcase their products and customers to easily discover traditional products from different districts of Tamil Nadu.

# 🚀 Key Features

## 👨‍🎨 Seller Features

Sellers can:

- 📝 Create a seller account
- 🔐 Login securely
- 🏪 Access a seller dashboard
- ➕ Add new products
- 🖼️ Upload product images
- 📦 Add product name and description
- 💰 Set product price
- 📍 Associate products with their district
- ✏️ Manage their products

---

## 🛍️ Buyer Features

Customers can:

- 📝 Create a buyer account
- 🔐 Login securely
- 🔎 Search for products
- 🏺 Browse traditional products
- 📍 Explore products by district
- 🖼️ View product images
- 📋 View product details
- 💰 Check product prices
- 🛒 Purchase products
